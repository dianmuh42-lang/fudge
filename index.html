Who is Fudge Jarcheh?

02 / 13

I don't believe creativity and entrepreneurship are different worlds.

Fudge Jarcheh works across business, technology, AI, music and creative projects. The output changes between them. The method does not: start from something that should exist, build it with taste, and judge it only by whether it reaches anyone.

That approach runs through Frequency Digital Group, the global parent entity behind his companies and ventures, and through Review Revolution, an AI-powered reputation platform built on the same principle. It started in music, as Fudge & The Frequency, where the idea was first proved.
